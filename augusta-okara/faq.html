faq

Questions readers ask.

Which of your books should I read first?

Start with The Salt Archive, my most recent novel. Every book stands alone, so you can read them in any order. If you would like a taste first, the short stories in Borrowed Light are a quick way in.

Are your books part of a series?

[Placeholder] Each novel is currently a standalone story, although they share some ideas and the occasional hidden connection for careful readers.

Will there be a sequel?

[Placeholder] I never say never. The best way to hear first is to join my reader list.

Where do your ideas come from?

Usually from a question that makes me uncomfortable. I read widely, history, science and the news, and pay attention when something feels like it is quietly shaping our future. Then I imagine where it leads in fifty or two hundred years.

Why science fiction for such serious themes?

Distance helps us see clearly. By moving a hard conversation to another planet or another century, readers can look at power, memory and identity without the defences they bring to the present day. Then they recognise it anyway.

Are your books suitable for younger readers?

[Placeholder] My novels are written for adults and older teens. Some books deal with violence, grief and loss. Check each book's details for content notes.

Do you read manuscripts or offer writing advice?

I am sorry, but I can't read unpublished manuscripts. I do share writing advice in my newsletter and at events, and I love speaking to writing groups.
