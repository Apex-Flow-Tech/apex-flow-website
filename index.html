From Leak to Fixed

Where enquiries leak, and what the right CRM does about it.

The four leaks we see most in growing teams, and how the system we build closes each one.

One inbox for every channel

LeakEnquiries arrive by WhatsApp, calls, Instagram, TikTok and your website, straight into staff members' personal phones.

FixEvery enquiry lands in one shared inbox the business owns, and is assigned to the right person automatically.

Follow-up you can actually see

LeakYou can't see who followed up and who didn't.

FixEvery enquiry gets an owner, a next step and a due date. Managers see what's done and what's overdue at a glance.

Reminders before customers go cold

LeakCustomers go cold and buy from someone else.

FixFollow-up messages and reminders go out on schedule, and stop the moment a customer replies.

Reports that show what's working

LeakYou can't tell which ads bring paying customers.

FixOne live dashboard shows enquiries by source, conversion at each stage and revenue won.
