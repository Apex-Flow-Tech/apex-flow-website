How We Work

One build standard. Every client, every time.

Apex Flow follows a fixed, five-stage sequence on every engagement: the same standard whether it's your first build or your fiftieth month on retainer. Nothing depends on memory, and any deviation gets documented before go-live.

01
Lead Capture & Qualification

Every lead lands in one queue, automatically.

We configure a dedicated intake queue and assignment rules so web and campaign leads route in without manual sorting. Source and channel are tagged on the way in, so your team knows what's working before they ever pick up the phone. A qualification bar keeps the pipeline honest, so nothing moves forward on a hunch.

02
Lead Conversion

One action, three clean records.

Converting a qualified lead generates an Account, Contact, and Opportunity in a single, standard action, with no custom conversion logic to maintain or break. From there, every deal moves through the same fixed path: Discovery → Proposal Sent → Negotiation.

03
Closing & Governance

Nothing closes without a sign-off.

An approval process sits on every Opportunity. Moving a deal to "Pending Sign-Off" triggers an automated approval request, and on approval, the deal auto-marks Closed-Won and locks. No silent edits after the fact, no deals that quietly slip through.

04
Delivery & Onboarding

The moment a deal closes, delivery starts itself.

A closed-won deal automatically spins up a delivery record connected to the account, with no manual handoff email required. Every existing client data source gets inventoried, field names and statuses get standardized before migration, and duplicates never make the trip. Legacy documents move to cloud storage and get linked, not uploaded, keeping the system light and fast.

05
Retainer & System Health

The system stays healthy after we hand it over.

Once a build ships, ownership shifts to ongoing care: a monthly system health check, a workflow audit to patch broken automations before they cause damage, and a data integrity pass. Support tickets are triaged inside a 24-hour SLA, with anything touching account health escalated immediately, never resolved as a one-off.

Built-In Guardrails

The constraints that keep a build honest.

Every Apex Flow system is engineered around the same non-negotiables, regardless of client size.

Lean license model

Roles are scoped to exactly what each job requires, so a small team can run a full system without paying for seats it doesn't need.

Storage-aware architecture

We treat storage limits as a hard constraint from day one: brief text notes on records, heavy files linked from cloud storage, never uploaded direct.

Documented deviations

If a build has to deviate from the standard, it's written down and flagged before go-live, never left as an undocumented exception.
