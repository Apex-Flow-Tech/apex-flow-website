What We See in Abuja Real Estate

Three ways your pipeline is leaking right now.

Not hypothetical. This is the same pattern across nearly every developer and agency we've evaluated in the CBD, Wuse 2, Jabi, Guzape, and Maitama corridor.

Leads die in WhatsApp

Your ad spend generates hundreds of leads a month. They land in an individual agent's personal chat, not a shared system. If they don't follow up that day, nobody else knows the lead exists.

Agents take the client list when they leave

There's no central database. Every relationship an agent built, every "call me when the next phase launches," walks out the door with them. The business owns the ad spend; the agent owns the outcome.

Deals go cold in month two

Property deals close over 1–6 months. Without automated reminders, a warm lead that isn't touched in week three is a cold lead by week six, and nobody flags it until it's already lost.

How We Fix It

One system, built around how your team actually sells.

Same five-stage build standard we run for every client, adapted to the way property deals actually move.

Ad leads land in one queue, not one phone

Instagram and Facebook leads route into a shared queue automatically, tagged by campaign and district, and assigned to an agent by rule, not by who happens to check their phone first.

A central client database the business owns

Every client, every unit of interest, every conversation history lives in one system the company controls. When an agent leaves, their pipeline transfers to the team, not out the door.

Automated nurture and follow-up reminders

A deal in week three gets a system-generated nudge before it goes cold, so follow-up doesn't depend on one agent remembering out of a hundred open conversations.

Pipeline visibility for the MD, in real time

See exactly which units are hot, which are stalled, and which agent is holding what, without asking for a manual update.
