My story

[Placeholder: where Augusta grew up, and the books, people or moments that made her a reader.]

I fell in love with science fiction because it let me ask dangerous questions in safe places. What if memory could be bought? What if a whole people could be voted out of history? What if the machines we build learn our worst habits first?

My novels and stories follow ordinary people, archivists, couriers, daughters and grandmothers, who find themselves at the centre of very large forces. I care less about the gadgets than what they reveal about us: who gets protected, who gets left behind, and who decides.

I am a storyteller, a daughter, a witness and a dreamer.

I write about the futures we are building and the people they leave behind. Every story asks one question: who gets to belong tomorrow?
