Tomorrow is a question.

I'm Augusta Okara. I write science fiction about power, memory and belonging: stories set in tomorrow that are really about the questions we keep avoiding today.

What the stories are about

Big ideas. Human stakes.

Power

Who builds the future, who profits from it, and who pays the bill.

Memory

What a people chooses to remember, and what is taken from them.

Identity

Bodies, borders and the selves we are allowed to become.

Resistance

Ordinary people refusing the story they were handed.
