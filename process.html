How We Work

One build standard. Every client, every time.

Apex Flow follows a fixed, five-stage sequence on every engagement: the same standard whether it's your first build or your fiftieth month on retainer. Nothing depends on memory, and any deviation gets documented before go-live.

Lead Capture & Qualification

Every lead lands in one queue, automatically.
  • Dedicated intake queue
  • Automatic assignment rules
  • Source & channel tagged at intake

Lead Conversion

One action, three clean records.
  • Account, Contact & Opportunity created
  • No custom conversion logic
  • Standard deal path: Discovery → Proposal Sent → Negotiation

Closing & Governance

Nothing closes without a sign-off.
  • Approval process on every Opportunity
  • "Pending Sign-Off" triggers approval request
  • On approval, auto Closed-Won & locked

Delivery & Onboarding

The moment a deal closes, delivery starts itself.
  • Delivery record auto-created & linked to account
  • Data inventoried, fields & statuses standardized
  • De-duplicated; documents linked from cloud storage

Retainer & System Health

The system stays healthy after we hand it over.
  • Monthly system health check
  • Workflow audit & automation patching
  • 24-hour SLA for support tickets
Built-In Guardrails

Precise License Model

Roles are scoped to exactly what each job requires, so you only pay for the seats you actually need, whether that's five or five hundred.

Storage-Aware Architecture

We treat storage limits as a hard constraint from day one: brief text notes on records, heavy files linked from cloud storage.

Documented Deviations

If a build has to deviate from the standard, it's written down and flagged before go-live, never left as an undocumented exception.
