Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

Why isn't pricing listed as a flat number?

Every build is scoped to your license count, pipeline complexity, and data volume. A five-person team and a five-hundred-person team need different architectures. A strategy call gets you an exact quote fast.

Can I start with a Launch Sprint and add a retainer later?

Yes, that's the standard path. Most clients launch first, then move to a retainer once the system is live and generating day-to-day usage worth maintaining.

What happens if my data storage grows past the limit?

We architect for storage guardrails from day one: brief text notes on records, heavy files linked from cloud storage, so most clients don't hit the ceiling. If you're close, we'll flag it before it's a problem.

Do you work outside of Salesforce?

Our core architecture practice is Salesforce-native. If your stack is different, tell us on the strategy call and we'll be straight with you about fit.

Is pricing in naira or dollars?

Nigeria-based clients are scoped and invoiced in naira, no dollar-denominated line items, no exchange-rate guessing games on your budget. Clients outside Nigeria are quoted in USD instead.
