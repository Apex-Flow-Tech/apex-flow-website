Best CRM Implementation Partners for Nigerian Property Sales Teams (2026)

The short answer: the right partner is the one that can show your own sales stages working in a demo, from WhatsApp enquiry to inspection, payment plan and allocation, and that will still support you after launch. Here's how the main options in Nigeria compare.

A note on fairness: this guide is written by Apex Flow Technologies, a CRM implementation partner and official Salesforce Partner based in Abuja. We've included ourselves, and we've described every other firm only from what its own listings and public profiles say, with links so you can check. Judge us by the same checklist.

What a Nigerian property CRM has to handle

Before comparing firms, be clear on the job. A CRM for a developer, agency or brokerage in Lagos or Abuja needs to cover:

  • WhatsApp-first leads: enquiries from WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook ads, property portals such as PropertyPro and Nigeria Property Centre, walk-ins and referrals, all in one queue the company owns.
  • Units and plots: estates, phases, units or plots with live status (available, reserved, allocated, sold).
  • Site inspections: booked, reminded and followed up, because that's where buyers are won or lost.
  • Off-plan payment plans in naira: instalment schedules, reminders and balances.
  • Documents: offer letters, allocation letters, contracts and title documents tied to each buyer.
  • Agents and brokers: automatic assignment, commissions, and client history that stays with the business when an agent leaves.

How to choose: the 6 questions to ask every partner

  1. Can you demo our own stages: enquiry, inspection, offer, payment plan, allocation?
  2. How do WhatsApp and portal enquiries get into the CRM, and who owns them?
  3. How do you track units, plots and instalments in naira?
  4. What happens to our clients and data when an agent leaves?
  5. Who supports us after launch, where are they based, and how fast do they respond?
  6. What's the full cost: setup, licences and monthly support?

For a longer list, see 12 questions to ask before you hire a CRM implementation partner.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best CRM implementation partner for Nigerian real estate companies?

It depends on your size and platform. Apex Flow Technologies, an Abuja-based official Salesforce Partner that also builds on HubSpot and Zoho, focuses on Lagos and Abuja developers and agencies with WhatsApp lead capture, site inspections and naira instalment plans. Larger Salesforce-only projects may suit a dedicated Salesforce consultancy, and small agencies may prefer a HubSpot partner such as Raflab.

Which CRM is best for real estate in Nigeria?

HubSpot suits smaller agencies that want an easy start, Zoho CRM suits budget-conscious teams that want strong WhatsApp integration, and Salesforce suits developers with several projects, brokers, payment plans and approvals. The setup matters more than the brand.

Can a CRM track off-plan instalments and allocations in Nigeria?

Yes. A properly set up CRM tracks units and plots with their status, instalment schedules in naira with payment reminders, and documents such as offer and allocation letters for each buyer.

How do I connect WhatsApp to a real estate CRM?

Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho CRM can all connect to WhatsApp Business through official integrations or partner tools, so chats from ads and portals land in the company CRM instead of an agent's personal phone.

How much does a real estate CRM implementation cost in Nigeria?

Costs depend on the platform, number of projects, data and integrations. Apex Flow Technologies quotes Nigerian clients in naira, with setup from 350,000 naira plus software licences at cost and an optional monthly support retainer.
