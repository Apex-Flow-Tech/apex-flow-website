The Salt Archive

  • Memory
  • Colonialism
  • Climate

Two hundred years after the coast went under, the Bureau of Recollection sells the memories of a lost city back to the descendants who can afford them. Ifeoma Daniels has spent her life cataloguing other people's grief. Then she finds a memory that was never meant to be kept: proof of who let the water in. A novel about inheritance, erasure and the price of remembering.

Children of the Quiet Orbit

  • Family
  • Faith
  • Survival

The ship has not heard from Earth in sixty years, and the elders say that is a mercy. Adaeze was born in the silence and has never questioned it, until the ship's oldest machine starts speaking to her in her grandmother's voice. A story about the stories communities tell to survive, and what happens when one girl stops believing them.
